The bundled files (assets/fonts/Geist-400/500/600.ttf and
assets/fonts/GeistMono-400/500/600.ttf) are static instances cut with
fontTools.varLib.instancer from the variable fonts published at
github.com/google/fonts (ofl/geist/Geist[wght].ttf and
ofl/geistmono/GeistMono[wght].ttf), pinned at the named weight. The family
declares no Reserved Font Name, so the instances keep the family names.
