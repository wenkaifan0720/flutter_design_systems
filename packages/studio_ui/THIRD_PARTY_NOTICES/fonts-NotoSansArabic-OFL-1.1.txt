The bundled files (assets/fonts/NotoSansArabic-400/700.ttf; Cody's pattern library carries 500 and 600 too) are cut
from the variable font google/fonts publishes as
ofl/notosansarabic/NotoSansArabic[wdth,wght].ttf at commit
8b0a1d0f5983c89bc2b93f1b5fb55f9e252744b5 (sha256
63111b5b2e074dd48cc67692e0a2726d86ee94c1c37fe8598257b7b4e87e869e): each is
the instance at that weight and the normal width, subset to the Arabic
blocks with every OpenType layout feature kept
(packages/drip_catalog/scripts/cody-script-faces.py in the drip repository).
The family declares no Reserved Font Name, so the modified files keep it;
the licence is google/fonts' ofl/notosansarabic/OFL.txt.
