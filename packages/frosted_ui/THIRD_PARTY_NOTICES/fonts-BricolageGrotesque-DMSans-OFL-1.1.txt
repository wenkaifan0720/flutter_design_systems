The bundled files are the variable fonts published at github.com/google/fonts,
copied byte for byte on 2026-10-31:

  assets/fonts/BricolageGrotesque-Variable.ttf
      ofl/bricolagegrotesque/BricolageGrotesque[opsz,wdth,wght].ttf
      (version 1.001; axes opsz 12-96, wdth 75-100, wght 200-800; the
      file's default instance is 96pt ExtraBold, so every FrostedThemeData style
      names its wght and opsz)
  assets/fonts/DMSans-Variable.ttf
      ofl/dmsans/DMSans[opsz,wght].ttf (version 4.004; axes opsz 9-40,
      wght 100-1000; default instance 9pt Regular)

They are the faces the look's source pages load from Google Fonts
(Bricolage Grotesque 400-800 at optical sizes 12-96, DM Sans 400-700 at
9-40), whole rather than subset. The licence texts are the families' OFL.txt
from the same directories. Neither family declares a Reserved Font Name.
