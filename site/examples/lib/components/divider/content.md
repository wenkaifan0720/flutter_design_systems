# Divider

A divider marks a boundary inside a short run of content. Keep the words on both sides meaningful even when the rule is invisible.

## Read a receipt

Read the subtotal above the divider and the total below. Add or remove the delivery fee to change the total.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab reaches the delivery action. The rule itself needs no keyboard stop.
- **Screen readers.** Read Subtotal and Total in order. Their labels explain the relationship without relying on the divider.
- **Direction and type.** The horizontal rule separates the same two lines in either direction. The amount and its label grow with the text setting.
