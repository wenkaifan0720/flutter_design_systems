# Tooltip

A tooltip gives a short hint about a control. Keep the action itself clearly named so the hint can add detail without carrying the instruction on its own.

## Explain a bookmark

Hover over Bookmark to reveal what saving the bookmark does. Try focusing it from the keyboard too. Activate the button to add the file, then activate it again to remove it.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the button to try its focus hint. Enter or Space toggles the bookmark.
- **Screen readers.** The button has a visible action name. Read its hint and the changed name after activation.
- **Direction and type.** The hint follows its anchor. Compare the available space around the button in RTL and at 200% text.
