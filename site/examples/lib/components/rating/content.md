# Rating

A rating records a judgment on a small, ordered scale. Name the thing being rated and show how many marks are available.

## Rate a guide

Choose a rating for the guide and read the score out of five.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Focus the rating. Use the arrow keys on a slider, or activate one of the individual stars. The score stays visible after focus leaves.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the name and current score, then move past the marks to the numeric result.
- **Direction and type.** Switch to RTL to inspect the order of the marks. The score below them follows the text scale.
