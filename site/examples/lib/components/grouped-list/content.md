# Grouped list

A grouped list puts related rows under one subject. Use it when the rows make more sense together than they would as separate cards.

## Choose a delivery channel

Choose Email or Post under Delivery. The line beneath the group shows which channel will be used.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab through the two actions and activate a channel. The current choice appears below the rows.
- **Screen readers.** Read the two choices in order, then continue to the line that names the chosen channel.
- **Direction and type.** The choices keep their vertical order in RTL. At larger text sizes check that both actions remain readable.
