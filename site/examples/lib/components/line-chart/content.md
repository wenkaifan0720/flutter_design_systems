# Line chart

A line chart joins measurements in order so changes in direction are easy to see. Keep the intervals consistent and name the units.

## Compare daily balances

Compare the balance over the first four days of January and February. January dips on day three, while February rises each day.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the period button and activate it. Where the chart accepts focus, try its arrow keys to inspect a point.
- **Screen readers.** Read the period and the chart’s description. The value or summary provided by the look changes with the data.
- **Direction and type.** The surrounding text follows reading direction. Check the plot’s own ordering, labels and legend in RTL and at larger type.
