# Segmented control

A segmented control offers a small set of mutually exclusive values. Keep the options short enough to compare at a glance.

## Choose a reporting period

Switch between Week and Month. The line below names the period included in the report.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the options, then follow their focus indicator to change the period. Some looks move between segments with the arrows.
- **Screen readers.** Read the segment names and inspect which option is selected. The example also prints the chosen period.
- **Direction and type.** In RTL the options follow reading order. Try the phone width with larger text to inspect both labels.
