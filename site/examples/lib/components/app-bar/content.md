# App bar

An app bar identifies the current screen and keeps its most relevant actions close. Save belongs here when it applies to the whole note.

## Save a note from the app bar

Use Save in the Notes bar. The status changes from Unsaved to Saved.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to Save and activate it. Read the result below the bar.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the bar’s title and the name of its action, then move past the bar to the saved state.
- **Direction and type.** Change direction to see which edge holds Save. At phone width and 200% text, inspect the space shared by the title and action.
