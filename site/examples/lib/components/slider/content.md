# Slider

A slider changes one value along a bounded track. Use it for adjustments people can judge as they move, such as sound level.

## Set the volume

Move the thumb and watch the volume readout change in five-point steps.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Focus the slider and use the arrows to adjust the volume. Try the same keys again in RTL.
- **Screen readers.** Read the control’s volume label and value, then the percentage printed beside the example.
- **Direction and type.** At 200% the number grows with the text. Switch direction to see where the low end of the track sits.
