# Sparkline

A sparkline gives a compact impression of change beside a value or a heading. Use a larger chart when readers need axes or exact comparisons.

## Read a five-day trend

Compare two weeks of requests. The first ends at eight requests and the second ends at four. Read the Monday and Friday counts alongside the small line.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the week button and activate it. A sparkline may be a single reading rather than a series of focus stops.
- **Screen readers.** The adjacent text names the week and gives its first and last counts. Compare it with the description the look gives its sparkline.
- **Direction and type.** The small plot sits beside the week’s reading. Check the direction of the line and the larger text separately.
