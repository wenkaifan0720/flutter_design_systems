# Bottom sheet

A bottom sheet gives a short task or a few details their own surface while keeping the current page in place.

## Read a delivery note

Open Delivery note to read where the parcel will be left. Close the sheet to return to the page.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to Delivery note and press Enter. Try Escape, then reopen the sheet and use its close action.
- **Screen readers.** Read from the sheet heading through the note and the dismiss action.
- **Direction and type.** Try larger type on the phone. On wider screens some looks present the same details on a centred surface.
