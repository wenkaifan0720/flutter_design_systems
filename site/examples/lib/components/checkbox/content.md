# Checkbox

A checkbox records an independent yes-or-no choice. A short action label makes it clear what checking the box will include.

## Request a receipt

Check Email receipt, then clear it. The summary changes to show whether a receipt was requested.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the checkbox and press Space to change its state. Press Space again to clear it.
- **Screen readers.** Read the label and checked state at the control. The sentence below describes the result of that choice.
- **Direction and type.** The label and result use the preview’s reading direction. Try larger text to inspect how they fit beside or below the checkbox.
