# Chips

A filter chip lets a reader apply or remove one condition without leaving the list. Its short label names the condition.

## Filter saved items

Turn Saved on to see the saved item, then turn it off to restore both items.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to Saved and activate it again to clear the filter.
- **Screen readers.** Read the chip’s name and selection state, then continue to the list to see which items remain.
- **Direction and type.** The chip precedes the items in both directions. Increase the text size to check the label alongside the results.
