# Radio group

Use radio buttons for one answer among a few visible alternatives. Keeping both choices in view makes their differences easy to compare.

## Choose a delivery method

Choose Pickup or Courier. Only one delivery method is current, and the confirmation repeats it.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Use Tab to enter the choices. Try the arrow keys in groups with roving focus, or Tab and Space on individually focusable radios.
- **Screen readers.** Each choice has a distinct name and a checked state. Read the confirmation after the group to hear the chosen delivery method.
- **Direction and type.** The options follow reading order. Try both directions and larger type on the phone to inspect the labels beside their controls.
