# Scroll area

A scroll area keeps a long sequence in a bounded part of the screen. Give the region a name and enough height to show where one item ends and the next begins.

## Browse the stops

Scroll from Stop 1 to Stop 12 and back. The surrounding page stays in place while the stops move.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab into the region. Try Arrow Down, Page Down and End to reach later stops, then Home to return.
- **Screen readers.** Read the region’s name, then browse the stops in sequence. Scrolling brings later stops into view.
- **Direction and type.** The sequence keeps its top-to-bottom order in RTL. At 200% fewer stops fit before you scroll.
