# Section header

A section header names the group that follows it. Put it immediately before the rows it describes so a reader can tell where a new group begins.

## Browse the agenda

Read today’s agenda, then switch to the other day. The heading and the appointment beneath it change together.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the day switch and activate it. The section title and its content are read together in document order.
- **Screen readers.** Read the day followed by its appointment. After switching days, check the new title in the same place.
- **Direction and type.** The heading follows the reading direction. Larger type shows how much room the title leaves for its content.
