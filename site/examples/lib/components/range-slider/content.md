# Range slider

A range slider sets a lower and upper bound together. It suits a filter where the distance between the two limits matters.

## Choose a price range

Adjust each end of the price filter. The readout gives the lowest and highest prices in dollars.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab through the two ends and use the arrows to adjust the focused value.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect each adjustable end separately. The price summary gives both values together.
- **Direction and type.** Switch direction to compare the two ends of the track. Try larger text while the bounds are close together.
