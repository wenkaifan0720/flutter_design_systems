# Inline calendar

An inline calendar keeps dates visible while a reader compares days. Use it when the month grid belongs on the page alongside a schedule.

## Choose a day on the calendar

Choose a day in November, then browse to December and choose another. The date below the calendar follows the selection.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Use the month controls to browse and focus a date to select it. Try the arrow keys in the day grid.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the month heading and day names, then read the selected date under the grid.
- **Direction and type.** Compare the two reading directions at phone width. In larger type the month grid can be scrolled into view.
