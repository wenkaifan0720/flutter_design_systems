# Disclosure

A disclosure lets readers open a short detail where they need it. Put enough information in the closed label for them to decide whether to expand it.

## Read pickup instructions

Open Pickup instructions to find the collection point. Close the section and the instructions disappear.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to Pickup instructions and activate it again to collapse it.
- **Screen readers.** Read the trigger, then move forward with it open to reach the instructions. Compare the expanded and collapsed states.
- **Direction and type.** The instructions use the same direction and text scale as their trigger. Try the phone width with larger type.
