# Toast or snackbar

A toast confirms a completed action without interrupting the next one. Put information the reader will need later on the page as well.

## Confirm a save

Press Save draft twice. Each confirmation includes its revision number, so you can see whether this look stacks, queues or replaces a message.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Activate Save draft from the keyboard and try a second save while the first message is visible.
- **Screen readers.** Listen for the saved revision. The trigger keeps its name while the message appears and disappears.
- **Direction and type.** Look at where the messages appear in RTL and whether their words fit at phone width with larger type.
