# Reorderable list

Use a reorderable list when the sequence belongs to the reader. Give each item a stable identity so moving a row keeps its text and focus together.

## Order the stops

Move Park below Market, then move it back. A row labelled Make first can also be activated to bring that stop to the top.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to a stop and activate Make first to move it to the start. Where the look handles Alt with Up or Down, those keys move the focused stop one position. The look’s note gives any other controls.
- **Screen readers.** Read the order of the stops after moving one. Check the move actions the look exposes on each item.
- **Direction and type.** The route still runs from top to bottom in RTL. Larger type should leave room for each stop and its handle.
