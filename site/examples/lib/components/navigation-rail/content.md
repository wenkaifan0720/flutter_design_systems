# Navigation rail

A navigation rail keeps a short list of destinations along the leading edge of a view, leaving room for the current page.

## Navigate along a rail

Choose Search on the rail to change the content beside it. Return to Home to restore the first page.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Focus the destinations and move down the rail. Activate Search if moving focus alone leaves Home selected.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the destination names and selected state, then read the content next to the rail.
- **Direction and type.** The rail sits at the reading start. Switch to RTL to move it to the other side of the content and try larger text at phone width.
