# Wheel picker

A wheel or stepped dial makes an ordered set of values easy to browse one at a time. It works best when nearby choices have a natural sequence.

## Choose a day with a wheel

Choose a day in November. Ledger supplies a date wheel, Appliance a knob with stops, and the other examples a short list of days.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Focus the wheel or dial and use the arrows to move between adjacent values.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the control’s day label and value. The text beneath it gives the selected day.
- **Direction and type.** In RTL the labels follow reading direction. Compare the current item with its neighbours at the larger text size.
