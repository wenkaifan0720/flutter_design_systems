# Date range picker

A date range picker takes two related dates, such as arrival and departure. Showing the interval helps the reader check which days are included.

## Book a date range

Choose the first and last days of a stay. Try another pair to replace the original booking.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the calendar or its opener. Use the day controls to choose both endpoints, confirming the choice when the look offers a confirmation action.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the day labels and the selected range. The booking summary prints both ends once the selection is complete.
- **Direction and type.** Try the month controls in RTL. At phone width and larger text, scroll within the example to reach the whole grid.
