# Card

A card keeps a small piece of content and its actions together. Give the group a subject that still makes sense when the card stands on its own.

## Pin a note

Pin the route note. Its action changes to Unpin so you can remove the pin again.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the action inside the card and activate it with Enter or Space.
- **Screen readers.** Read the note, then the action. After pinning, its name changes to Unpin.
- **Direction and type.** The note and its action stay together when the leading edge changes in RTL. Try the narrow preview at 200% to see the note wrap.
