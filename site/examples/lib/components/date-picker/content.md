# Date picker

Use a date picker when someone needs to choose a single day. A date a person already knows can also be entered in separate day, month and year fields.

## Set a due date

Set a due date in November. Some looks open a calendar from a field, others display it in place, and Civic takes the date as three numbers.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the field or calendar. Use the calendar’s month controls to browse, or type into each part of a date input.
- **Screen readers.** Inspect the day names, selected date or labelled date fields. Read the date in those fields or in the summary below the calendar.
- **Direction and type.** Switch direction to see how the date parts and month controls line up. At phone width, scroll the preview to reach a tall calendar.
