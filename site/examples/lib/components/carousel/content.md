# Carousel

Use a carousel when the reader can inspect a short sequence one item at a time. Keep the order stable and let the reader choose when to move.

## Browse the rooms

Move from the Hall to the Studio, then return. Try the carousel’s navigation as well as dragging the slides.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab into the carousel or its controls. Try its arrow keys or navigation buttons to move to the adjacent room.
- **Screen readers.** Read the room name and the position information the look provides as you move between slides.
- **Direction and type.** Check the direction of travel in RTL. Try the phone preview at 200% and read the room on each slide.
