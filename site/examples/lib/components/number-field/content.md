# Number field

A number field lets someone adjust a quantity in predictable steps. Set a meaningful lower bound, such as one copy of a document.

## Choose a quantity

Start with two copies. Add a copy, then lower the count to one and try to decrease it again.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the control. Depending on the look, use its increase and decrease actions or the Up and Down arrows on the value.
- **Screen readers.** Read the current quantity and the names of its adjustment controls. The total below the field also reports the number of copies.
- **Direction and type.** The adjustment controls follow the look’s reading order. Try the phone frame with larger type to inspect the number and its neighbours.
