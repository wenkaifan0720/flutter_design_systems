# Pie or donut chart

A pie or donut chart shows how a small number of categories divide a whole. Keep category names and amounts close to the slices.

## Compare budget shares

The budget totals 100 in both plans. Switch to Plan B to see ten move from Supplies to Travel while Fees stays at twenty.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to the plan button and activate it. In a look with keyboard selection on the chart, use the arrows to inspect slices.
- **Screen readers.** Read the period and the chart’s description. The value or summary provided by the look changes with the data.
- **Direction and type.** The surrounding text follows reading direction. Check the plot’s own ordering, labels and legend in RTL and at larger type.
