# Tag

A tag puts a short category or status next to an item. Give the status a word readers can understand even if they cannot distinguish its colour.

## Label a draft

The item starts as Draft. Publish it to change its tag, then return it to Draft.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Tab to Publish or Return to draft. The status tag itself is a label.
- **Screen readers.** Read Draft or Published before and after using the action. Both states have distinct words.
- **Direction and type.** The tag follows the surrounding reading direction. Try the longer Published label at the larger text size.
