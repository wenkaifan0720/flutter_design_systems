# Split button

A split button keeps a frequent action one press away and puts related alternatives in a menu. Name the first part so people know what will happen before opening the menu.

## Choose how to save

Choose Save for the current draft. Open the adjacent menu and choose Save a copy to see a different confirmation.

## Shared behaviour

- **Keyboard and focus.** Reach the primary action with Tab. Try the menu trigger, the arrow keys and Enter. Escape lets you leave a menu without choosing an action.
- **Screen readers.** The primary action and the menu trigger have separate names. The confirmation below the pair identifies which action ran.
- **Direction and type.** The menu trigger sits at the reading end of the pair. Open it with large text in the phone frame to inspect its alignment and label.
