Flutter design systems

Eight design systems for Flutter, one package each, built on one shared foundation, ui_core. Each one works under MaterialApp, CupertinoApp or WidgetsApp, at phone and desktop sizes, by day and by night.

Open a gallery to try every component in your browser, or add a package from pub.dev. Source and issues on GitHub.

Neumorphism

neumorphism_ui

Neumorphism for Flutter: soft raised surfaces, inset controls, five light and dark palettes, accessible interactions, role-based shapes and state-resolved styling.

Live gallerypub.devSource

Comic

comic_ui

A Flutter design system drawn as a printed comic: inked panels, speech balloons and everyday controls, five editions by day and night, with state motion.

Live gallerypub.devSource

Frosted

frosted_ui

Frosted glass widgets for Flutter: frosted panes on a canvas lit by soft washes of colour, seven light and dark palettes, accessible controls and a complete theme.

Live gallerypub.devSource

Ledger

ledger_ui

A Flutter design system kept in ink: warm paper, ink rules where other looks draw boxes, dot leaders, engraved figures and mono amounts, by day and by lamplight.

Live gallerypub.devSource

Minimal

minimal_ui

A precise, quiet design system for Flutter: hairlines, dense rows, mono tabular figures and one primary colour, in three presets by day and by night.

Live gallerypub.devSource

Fluent 2

fluent2_design_ui

Unofficial Fluent 2 widgets for Flutter: Fluent's tokens, type ramp and components, seven brand presets in light and dark, accessible controls.

Live gallerypub.devSource
