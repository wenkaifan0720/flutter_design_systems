Flutter design systems

Eight design systems for Flutter, one package each, built on one shared foundation, ui_core. Each one works under MaterialApp, CupertinoApp or WidgetsApp, at phone and desktop sizes, by day and by night.

Open a gallery to try every component in your browser, or add a package from pub.dev. Source and issues on GitHub.

Ledger

ledger_ui

A Flutter design system kept in ink: warm paper, ink rules where other looks draw boxes, dot leaders, engraved figures and mono amounts, by day and by lamplight.
